# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year. Leave accrues monthly and unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over
into the following year. Carryover must be used by 31 March.

## Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks. Requests must be submitted to a manager at
least 8 weeks before the intended start date.

## Approvals

All leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. The agent
provides policy information only and cannot approve or reject requests itself.
